Audio programming

The parts of a C# synthesizer that produce the sound, for a program that renders MIDI files to a WAV file sample by sample: oscillators, note frequencies, velocity and decay, polyphony and delay feedback.

C# · GXPEngine · 2016 · solo

Für Elise from a MIDI file, square wave, 16-bit 44.1 kHz (with sound)

Synthesis

The synthesizer is built on an existing C# audio framework. I implemented the parts that produce the sound: note numbers to equal-tempered frequencies, amplitude from note velocity, per-sample decay, the square, triangle and sawtooth oscillators, polyphony, feedback in the delay effect, and rendering a MIDI file's note-on and note-off events into the WAV file.

For polyphony the synth owns one mono synth per voice (16 here). A note-on takes the first free voice and is dropped when every voice is busy; a note-off frees the voice playing that note.
