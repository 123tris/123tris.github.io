Summary

I am a game programmer specializing in gameplay systems and developer tooling, working mostly in C# and Unity, with a background in C++ engine development. I like building the frameworks and editors that make the rest of a team faster, and then shipping games with them.

At XR Japan in Tokyo I lead programming on Lumen, a virtual endoscopy trainer in which doctors practise endoscopic surgery with a custom controller, as well as VR and iPadOS applications for other clients: architecture, simulation and performance work, shared libraries and tools, technical discussions with clients and mentoring the team. Outside work I co-founded Idomi Games, where I program Ryze, a precision platformer on Steam that began as a Global Game Jam entry, and I maintain Ripple, an open-source ScriptableObject framework.
